Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 4 September 2026

This is a standard starting-point template, not reviewed by a lawyer. Read it before publishing, adjust anything that doesn't match reality (the contact address in particular), and have someone qualified check it if you want a real compliance guarantee — especially for EU/UK visitors.

Engineer Booklet ("we," "this site") is a reference site for the Engineer Booklet YouTube channel. This page explains what data the site collects, how cookies are used, and how third parties — mainly Google — are involved.

What this site collects directly

The site itself has no accounts, no forms, and no server-side database — it's a static site. The only thing it stores in your browser directly is a small amount of local storage for your own convenience: your light/dark theme choice and your cookie-consent choice. Neither ever leaves your browser or gets sent to us.

The hosting provider keeps standard server access logs (IP address, page requested, timestamp, user agent) for security and operational purposes, the way virtually all web hosts do.

YouTube embeds

Video pages embed YouTube via youtube-nocookie.com, which limits cookies until you actually press play. Once you play a video, YouTube (Google) may set cookies and collect data under its own privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses, or intends to use, Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites. You can review or opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and read more about how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland see a consent banner before any ad-related cookie is set, and can withdraw consent at any time by clearing this site's local storage and reloading the page.

Cookies used here, in plain terms

  • Theme & consent choice — local storage, not a cookie technically, set by us, never expires until you clear it.
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google/AdSense only after you accept, used to personalize and measure ads.
  • YouTube cookies — set by Google once you play an embedded video, governed by YouTube's own policy, not ours.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this page

If what this site collects or how it uses ads changes materially, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.
